## Current experience

The game now renders a ring-shaped ship in three.js, retaining the underlying room logic and a painted fallback. Seven spaces are reachable: corridor, bridge, greenhouse, radio room, galley, observation dome and gangway. The delivery loop takes a radio request through choosing a destination, growing and assembling a living lamp, docking, offering the lamp and letting go. The visitor carries the lamp to its place; the ship leaves and its light remains outside. The first request and one flowering plant are prepared for the first voyage.

Composed viewpoints, limited head movement and leaning bring objects within reach. The sea chart and the outside scenery use shared voyage state. Paper instructions and ink marks carry the work into the world. English is the default; Chinese remains available. Sound can be declined before boarding, reduced motion is supported, and the standalone build saves locally.
